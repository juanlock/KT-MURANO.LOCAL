Murano Business Suite · User manual

Murano, by company and role

What you see in Murano depends on two things: the company you belong to and the role your administrator gave you. Find your role below, read what it can do, then follow the guides linked from it.

For everyone

How Murano works for every user

One login, one company

Your login belongs to one company. You only ever see that company's customers, sales, employees and files. Only the system owner (SuperAdmin) can switch between companies.

Your role decides the menu

Menus, buttons and even single tabs appear only if your role allows them. If you need something you can't see, ask your company administrator to add it to your role. Nothing is given by job title alone.

Your home page

Some roles open straight on their work screen instead of the dashboard: field crews on Daily Tasks, salon staff on the Bookings calendar.

The top bar

  • Book: the manual inside Murano, for the screen you are on or the whole manual, in English or Spanish.
  • Clock: clock in and out (if your role has it).
  • Language: English or Spanish.
  • Your name: Profile, password, sign out.

Your company can hide icons; you can also show or hide them in your Profile.

Phones and tablets

Murano runs in the browser. The menu folds into the ☰ button, lists show the important columns first, and date filters turn into a dropdown.

When someone leaves

When an employee is removed, their login is disabled and they are signed out on every device at once. Their history (sales, jobs, attendance) is kept.

System owner

SuperAdmin

The owner of the whole installation. Not tied to one company: works on any of them through the company selector.

Step by step

Guides

Each guide says which roles use it. If a menu item in a guide is missing for you, your role doesn't include it.

Sign in and your home page

For: everyone

  1. Open https://murano.kcoltech.com and sign in with your email and password.
  2. Murano opens your role's home page: the Dashboard, Daily Tasks (Ruba crews) or the Bookings calendar (New Era).
  3. Use the left menu (☰ on a phone) to move around. The logo takes you back to your home page.
  4. Sign out from your name in the top bar. On a shared computer, always sign out.
Forgot your password, or your login says it is disabled? Ask your company administrator. Employee logins are active only after the company sets a password for them.

Profile, password, language and PIN

For: everyone

  1. Click your name in the top bar › Profile.
  2. Change your password, photo and contact details, then Save.
  3. Under Header icons, choose for each icon: Company default, Show or Hide.
  4. Switch the language (English / Spanish) with the language icon in the top bar.
Your PIN (4 to 8 digits, unique in your company) is set by your administrator in Users. It identifies you at the shared register. Never share it.
Back to top

Clock in and clock out

For: ManagerNE, StaffNE, and any role with "clock in"

  1. When your shift starts, click the clock icon in the top bar. It turns green and shows the time you clocked in.
  2. When you leave, click it again. Murano shows the time and the hours worked today.
  3. Forgot to clock? Tell your manager; they can correct it in HRM › Attendance.

Daily Tasks (field crews)

For: OperatorRG, SupervisorRG, Manager RG

  1. After sign-in, Daily Tasks opens by itself. It lists the jobs for today; switch to the week to plan ahead.
  2. When you arrive at the customer, tap Start on that job.
  3. When you finish, tap Complete, write a short note of what was done and enter the hours worked.
  4. Supervisors see every crew's jobs and can assign a job to a technician from the same screen.
Your weekly pay is calculated from the hours you enter on completed jobs, so complete every job the same day.
Back to top

Service jobs

For: Manager RG, SupervisorRG, KcolTech Admin-ALL

  1. Go to Service & Maintenance › All Jobs (or Today's Jobs).
  2. Create a job: customer, date, the work to do, and the technician or crew.
  3. From a sale: in Sales › Add Sale, press Add Service Job to create the job linked to that invoice. Pending and completed jobs show different icons.
  4. Record the equipment used on the job; the job cost adds labor hours and equipment.
  5. Follow up unpaid work in Awaiting Payment.
Back to top

Sales and invoices

For: administrators, Manager RG, ManagerNE (view, add), Sales Agents

  1. Sales › Add Sale: choose date, customer and location, add products or services, then save. The number comes automatically from your company's series (KcolTech and Ruba INV_…, New Era SL…).
  2. Sales › List: filter by date, customer or status. On a phone the list shows the sale information first.
  3. Open a sale to add a payment, print it or email it to the customer.
  4. The Other due column shows the customer's other unpaid invoices. Open it to see each invoice's date and how many reminder emails were sent and when.
Back to top

Sales contracts (recurring billing)

For: KcolTech and Ruba administrators, Manager RG

  1. Sales › Sales Contracts › Add.
  2. Pick the customer, the services, the frequency (weekly or monthly) and the start date.
  3. Send the contract for signature.
  4. Murano creates the invoices and the scheduled service visits on each date; check them in the Sales list and in Daily Tasks.

POS and the cash register

For: StaffNE, ManagerNE, administrators

  1. At the start of the day the manager opens the register (POS › open register) with their PIN and the opening cash.
  2. To sell: open POS, enter your PIN so the sale is recorded for you, add the services and products, and choose the customer (or Walk-in).
  3. Take the payment. A sale can be split between cash and card.
  4. Print or email the receipt.
  5. At closing the manager closes the register with their PIN and checks the count against the Cash Register Report.
Commission payroll counts the sales recorded under your PIN, or the bookings where you are the stylist.

Bookings calendar

For: StaffNE (own), ManagerNE and AdminNE (everyone)

  1. The calendar opens after sign-in. Switch between day, week and month.
  2. Click an empty slot to add a booking: customer, service, stylist and time.
  3. Online bookings arrive as Pending. Open one and Confirm it; the customer receives the confirmation email (if they gave an email).
  4. When the customer is served, charge the booking at the POS.
Back to top

Online booking on your website

For: AdminNE, administrators

  1. Booking Management › Online Booking.
  2. Adjust the look: show or hide the logo, background color.
  3. Under Websites & codes, add each website that shows the form. Murano gives you the code to paste: an embedded form, a link or a button.
  4. Customers book in 3 steps; only the name is required. Admins receive the "new online booking" email; the customer is emailed only when you confirm.
  5. "Exit" and "Close" take the customer back to the website the code belongs to.
Spam protection: at most 5 bookings every 10 minutes from the same connection. From the 4th booking in 24 hours the customer must give an email and click the "I agree" link we send.

Customers

For: administrators, Manager RG, ManagerNE

  1. People › Customers › Add. Enter name, email and phone.
  2. One email = one customer in your company. Before adding, search by email to avoid duplicates.
  3. A customer with an email gets a login to the customer portal.
  4. Open a customer to see their sales, payments, bookings and balance.
Back to top

Employees

For: administrators, Manager RG (others see their own record)

  1. HRM › Employees › Add. Every employee gets a login automatically; it is active when the company has a default employee password (System Settings › Defaults › Users).
  2. On the employee page, use the tabs: basic data, company, accounts, user, production, payments and Assets assigned.
  3. Set the pay type: hourly (with the rate) or commission (with the %), and the date it starts.
  4. Print profile prints the whole record with the photo.
  5. Remove an employee: Murano disables the login, signs them out everywhere, unassigns their open jobs, upcoming bookings and tickets, and lists the assets to collect. Their history stays.

Weekly payroll

For: administrators with the Payroll permission

  1. HRM › Payroll › Generate weekly payroll, and choose the week.
  2. Hourly employees: hours on the jobs they completed that week × their rate. Commission employees: their sales that week × their %.
  3. Review each line, then mark it paid. Paid payrolls go to accounting automatically (salaries, or commissions).

Email Office

For: roles with Email Office (Manager RG reads; ManagerNE and administrators also manage it)

  1. Email Office › Received or Sent.
  2. Open a message to read it and see its attachments; a message is linked to its customer or supplier when the address matches.
  3. Payment emails (for example Zelle) are detected and can be matched to a customer.
  4. Managers set the mailbox, folders and rules in Email Office › Settings.

Website

For: administrators, Manager RG

  1. Website Management › Website Settings: website address, name, logo, front page and menus.
  2. An imported website is edited in Website › Pages: texts, pictures, links and Sections.
  3. In a section, Use our booking calendar puts Murano's booking form in place of an old one.
  4. Before an import replaces your site, Murano keeps a copy in Saved websites (Preview, Use this, Delete).
  5. Gallery and Portfolio have their own menus for photos and projects.

Users and roles

For: administrators (Manager RG can edit, not add)

  1. User Management › Users › Add creates admin logins only. Employees, customers, suppliers and sales agents get their login from their own module.
  2. Edit any login here: role, locations, password, PIN, and the Active / Disabled switch. Disabling signs the person out everywhere. You can't disable yourself.
  3. Roles: tick what the role can do, module by module and tab by tab, and choose its Home page.
  4. Header icons for the whole company: System Settings › Defaults › Users.
Back to top

Date filters and reports

For: everyone who sees lists or reports

  1. Every list and report has the same date filter: All, Today, This Week … Last Year, plus Start date and End date you can type in.
  2. Double-click a preset to make it your default (★); the page opens with it next time.
  3. Reports export to PDF or Excel from the buttons above the table.

Help desk tickets

For: everyone (My tickets); Helpdesk role and administrators (all tickets)

  1. Help desk › My tickets › New: describe the problem and attach a screenshot.
  2. Follow the answers in the same ticket.
  3. Help desk staff work from Help desk › Tickets and the dashboard.
Back to top

Network security

For: roles with "Network security" (company admins). On for KcolTech.

  1. Settings › Network security: turn on Restrict access by network and add the office's public IP (or a range). Add it puts your current IP in the list.
  2. Choose what users follow: Restricted or Anywhere. Under People, set anyone on their own (a field worker on phone data: Can sign in from anywhere).
  3. A restricted user who signs in from outside is stopped, and that computer shows up in Computers and in the bell. Approve it once and it works from anywhere; Remove it when a laptop is lost or someone leaves.
Only the admin panel is restricted. The store, websites, portfolio, online booking and the portals stay open, and the SuperAdmin is never blocked.

Customer and supplier portal

For: customers and suppliers

  1. Sign in at https://murano.kcoltech.com with the email the company has on file.
  2. Customers see their invoices, payments, balance and bookings. Suppliers see their purchase orders and payments.
  3. Need a change to your details? Contact the company directly.
