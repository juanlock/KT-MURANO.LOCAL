Let a sales contract create service jobs (e.g. weekly maintenance visits) automatically, configured on the contract create/edit form.
Solution

Done. Sales Contracts can now create Service Jobs automatically, as recurring visits.

On Sales Contracts → New there's a new card, "Service jobs (visits)", under "Recurring invoices". Turn on "Create service jobs automatically" and set:

  • Visit title, with the same placeholders as the invoice notes, for example Lawn mowing - {{weekday}} {{invoice_date}} (visit {{number}}). Empty = the contract subject.
  • Technician / crew lead (only your company's technicians), job type and visit time.
  • Create ahead: how many days before each visit the job is created (default 7), so the crew sees it in advance.
  • Its own schedule, separate from the invoices: every N weeks / months, which day (e.g. every Tuesday), how many visits, and the first visit date. A live preview lists the visits with their titles filled in.

So a contract can be billed monthly with weekly visits.

What each visit creates: a normal Service Job:

  • the contract's customer and location
  • the technician, the title, status Pending, and the visit date and time
  • no price (the contract's invoices bill the work, so nothing is billed twice)

It shows up in Service Jobs like any other job.

When the jobs are created:

  • When you save an Active contract, the visits already within the "create ahead" window are created right away.
  • Every morning, the daily contracts run (sales-contracts:invoice) creates the next ones.
  • Each visit is created once (deleting one doesn't recreate it). It stops at the number of visits or the end date, and pauses while the contract isn't Active.

On the contract's page: a "Service jobs (visits)" summary (next visit, visits created, technician) and a "Service jobs created" table with links to each job.

The card only appears for companies with the Service module on: Ruba, KcolTech and Hartfield. If a company has it off, the server refuses.

On the sale's Service job card ([/next/sales/3922/edit](http://kt-murano.local/next/sales/3922/edit), and on Create Sale), choose Repeat and set:

  • First date: empty = the sale's date.
  • Every: e.g. 1 week, 2 weeks, or 1 month.

  • Recurring (on Create Sale, or a Sales Contract): ongoing work billed every period, where the invoices and the visits keep coming automatically.
