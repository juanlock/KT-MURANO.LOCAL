TRAVELER-FRIENDLY, RELIABLE SERVICE:

With Westchester Taxi & Limo Airport Service. We are located in the Westchester area, just minutes away from JFK, Newark and La Guardia airports.

Our Airport Taxi & limo Service is professional, dependable and reliable. We provide airport Taxi & limo service to all major New York City airports; La Guardia airport, JFK ( Kennedy ) airport, Newark airport, Macarthur airport and Bradley airport. Each of our limos and sedans are chauffeured by a professional driver, with your safety, comfort, needs and desires in mind.

Our Taxi or limo will pick you up anywhere in the New York Tri-State area and take to the airport of your choice. We operate every day to and from the airports no matter how late or early the flight leaves or arrives, our airport limo service will be there for you. We track all flights for delays and early & late arrivals, including canceled flights.

On arriving flights, chauffeurs are expected to be at the airport by the pre-flight arrival time and will hold an 8.5” by 11” laminated sign that reads: “Westchester Taxi & Limo Airport Service Welcomes…” followed by the passengers surname. At the airport terminal we’ll introduce ourselves and assist with the passengers’ luggage and take them safely and comfortably to their destination.

We offer door-to-door service directly to and from the lobbies of all major Hotels. To make a reservation and schedule an airport limo pickup time please call us at (914) 946-9333. We are glad to be of service.

If you feel your rights were violated by a taxicab driver please File a Complaint. Thank you
