AIRPORT ARRIVAL PROCEDURE & CHARGES

HOME >

Westchester Taxi and Limo

For pre-arranged airport arrivals, the following procedure will apply:

Cancellation Policy: Due to the long distances nature of a lot of trips, Customers are kindly requested to inform us with the changes or cancellations on international flights 4 Hours ahead of time, on Domestic flights 2 hours ahead of time.

A) We will charge1/2 the trip fare if cancellation is only in an hour time ahead of pickup time.

B) We will charge full fare if the cancellations is in less than half an hour including No Show (please see No show procedure below)

Domestic and International Arrivals: Driver will meet passenger outside at passenger pick up area with a sign with the passenger’s name, unless is requested we will pickup inside. There is a 30-minutes grace period from scheduled arrival for domestic flights and 30-minutes for international arrivals. After grace period, waiting time charges will apply $25.00 for the first 1/2 hour & $ 50.00 for each additional hour.

Wait Time (applies to point-to-point pick ups) Wait time is $30.00 per hour. There is a 15-minute grace period from scheduled pick-up time. (This does not apply to airport pick- ups, please see arrivals procedure below.) After the grace period, wait time will be billed in 15-minute increments at $10 per 15-minute interval.

No-Show Charge: A no-show will be billed when our driver is on location for pick-up and the passenger does not show. A no-show will be charged when there is no record of cancellation or the passenger leaves pick-up location without contacting our office. The charge for a no-show will be the full fare of the service. Passengers arriving at Any Airport are advised to proceed at passenger pick- up and contact our office.

Additional Passenger Charge: $10.00 Each additional passenger Children under 6 years are free.

Holiday Charges A $20.00 holiday fee will be added to all reservations on the following holidays:

  • New Years Day
  • Memorial Day
  • 4th of July
  • Labor Day
  • Thanksgiving
  • Christmas Day

Early Morning/Late Night Pick-up Charges A $10 fee will be added to all pick-ups made between 1:00 AM and 4:00 AM

Additional Stops Additional stops made en-route to destination will be billed as follows:

  • Same town $10.00 per stop
  • Different towns, same county $20.00 per stop
  • Different Counties $30.00 per stop

Parking Fee All pre-arranged pick-ups with sign airport arrivals will be charge parking fee.

Tolls All toll charges will be billed round-trip.

Gratuity Gratuity will be 20% for all services.

Hourly Rates Hourly Minimum Sedan $65.00 per hour 3 hour minimum

The above rates do not include 20%Gratuity, Tolls and Parking where it applies

Owner-Operator & Employment Opportunities Westchester Taxi and Limo is proud to have Owner-Operator drivers

Today, Westchester Taxi and Limo Owner-Operators form the core of our driving staff. An Owner-Operator, with their vested interest in our business, provides a higher level of service than the typical employee driver. It has to do with the pride-in-ownership that goes along with being an Owner-Operator.
Owner-Operators have the complete support of Westchester Taxi and Limo Inc. A new Owner-Operator goes through a comprehensive training program and quickly becomes an expert in airport operation and customer service.
Contrary to what many people think, previous airport transportation experience is not a key element in making a successful Owner-Operator. What they have in common is a desire for independence and a love for both driving and interacting with people.
As a Westchester Taxi and Limo Owner-Operator you are in business for yourself, but not by yourself.
A small investment is required to become an Owner-Operator and a good income is available to all who enjoy people and driving.

Driver Positions:

  • Minimum 21 years old.
  • Proficient in written and spoken English.
  • Motivated individuals with a strong knowledge of our service area.
  • Must be personable and customer service oriented.
  • Current DMV printout indicating a safe driving record.
